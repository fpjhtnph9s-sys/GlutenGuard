Before you start

GlutenGuard checks products against public food databases to flag likely gluten content. Please keep in mind:

  • Database entries are crowd-sourced and can be outdated, incomplete, or wrong.
  • Manufacturers reformulate products without notice.
  • A "no gluten listed" result is not a certified guarantee — always check the physical label.
  • If a result and the label ever disagree, trust the label. When you're not sure, don't eat it.
  • This app is not medical advice.
